## Description

The pricing job scheduled the FX snapshot five minutes early to give the NAV calculation more
headroom. Funds with USD and JPY share classes were valued with stale rates. The impact per share
class was below the 0.5% materiality threshold, so no restatement was required.

## Proposed fix

Trigger the snapshot from the WM/Reuters fix publication event rather than a cron schedule.
See [FX rate sourcing](../../../confluence/spaces/NAV/fx-rates-sourcing.md).

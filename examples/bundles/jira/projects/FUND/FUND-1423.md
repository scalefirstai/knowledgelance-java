# FUND-1423: Late trades missed the 16:00 NAV cut-off

## Description

On 2026-10-09 eleven equity trades for funds LU-EQ-01, LU-EQ-04 and IE-EQ-07 were booked by the
dealing desk at 16:07 CET. The order management system stamped them with trade date T, so the
fund accounting platform included them in the T NAV strike. Per the prospectus they should have
been treated as late trades and rolled to T+1.

## Root cause

The OMS cut-off check used the desk's local time zone (London) instead of CET. Between the clock
change weekends the one-hour difference let trades through until 17:00 CET.

## Resolution

- Cut-off check moved to the fund accounting platform and evaluated in CET.
- Late-trade exception queue added; see the [NAV cut-off runbook](../../../confluence/spaces/NAV/nav-cutoff-runbook.md).
- Restatement approved under [decision record](../../../confluence/spaces/NAV/late-trades-decision.md).
